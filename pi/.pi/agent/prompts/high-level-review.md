# High Level Review

Review the codebase at a high level, focusing on architecture, abstractions, and overall readability rather than correctness or performance.

Evaluate whether the code is easy for an experienced engineer to understand and modify. In particular, look for:

Clear module boundaries and separation of concerns.
Good abstractions with high cohesion and low coupling.
Functions that do one thing well and maintain a consistent level of abstraction.
Simple control flow with minimal duplication and unnecessary indirection.
Clear, descriptive names for functions, variables, types, and modules.
Organization that broadly follows clean-code principles, applied pragmatically rather than dogmatically.
Tasteful comments that explain intent or non-obvious decisions, not the code itself.

Identify the highest-impact design issues, explain why they matter, and suggest concrete improvements. Prioritize substantive findings over style nits, distinguish objective issues from subjective preferences, and avoid recommending abstractions that add more complexity than they remove.
